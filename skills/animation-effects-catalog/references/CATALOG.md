# CATALOG — tricks that make AI animation impressive

Format: **Name** — when to use · `copy-ready phrase` (adapt nouns, keep the mechanism).

## A. BY HERO TYPE

### A1. Tiny creatures / miniature people (fairies, mini-angels, a 15 cm human)
- **Scale by props, not by numbers alone** — always · `each about 8 cm tall; the sugar cube is as big as her head, the teaspoon almost twice her height, the cup like a large bath beside her`
- **Carried weight changes flight** — whenever a tiny figure carries something · `she carries the cube hugged in both arms, flying low and heavy under its weight; when she lets go she bobs upward, suddenly light`
- **Teamwork on a human-sized task** — comedy/charm · `she grips the top of the spoon handle and flies small circles dragging it; the second one pushes the handle from behind`
- **Tiny bodies disturb the big world** — sells presence · `their wingbeats stir the steam`; `a few drops fly from the splash`
- **Macro realism, not toy** — always · `real skin, real hair, real fabric at macro detail; no doll or figurine look, no cartoon look, no sparkles, no magic glow`
- **Tiny-world sound** — always · `the fine high hum of tiny wings, the plop of the cube, the spoon ringing softly against porcelain`
- **The big human holds still for them** — when a normal-size person shares the frame · `her job is to hold the cup dead still for them; her eyes follow each one from above, careful and fond`

### A2. Giants vs a tiny human (real-size pets towering over a 15 cm man, a giant paw entering a street)
- **Giant part as a soft, motion-blurred mass** — the giant enters the frame · `a giant paw comes down from the top of frame, bigger than his whole body, soft and slightly motion-blurred, real fur strands`
- **Camera at the tiny one's eye level** — always · `camera placed low at the tiny man's eye level inside the miniature world; the giant is out of focus beyond`
- **Faces as a sky** — POV of the tiny one · `two enormous faces fill the opening above like a sky`
- **Rescue reads only if the giant is ENORMOUS** — lift/carry beats · `the cat's head alone is bigger than the man's whole body; it holds him up in front of its face like a toy`
- **Scale change on contact** — returning to normal size · `the moment his boots touch the rug he grows smoothly to full human size; slow motion only on the growth`

### A3. Real animals as characters (costumed pets, deadpan)
- **Real pet, real costume** — always · `real living pets filmed in camera, real fur, weight and anatomy; their small clothes are real pet garments sitting slightly loose, fur poking out at the cuffs; never on two legs, no human hands`
- **One specific behaviour per animal** — animals as audience/background · `the dog follows the bow arm and tilts its head once; the cat narrows its eyes, one ear turned to the sound; the bird makes one tiny hop`
- **Stillness is never a freeze** — calm animal shots · `calm breathing, blinking and subtle fur movement; stillness never becomes a frozen image`
- **Ears and whiskers act** — instead of human faces · `ears swivel toward the sound, whiskers twitch, tail flicks — expression only through ears, eyes, whiskers and posture`
- **Deadpan** — comedy · `nobody acknowledges that the therapist is a cat`

### A4. Surreal anatomy / human-sized animal (a giraffe with a 6 m neck at a desk)
- **Fully formed in frame one** — always · `the neck is already this length and shape in the first frame and stays anatomically continuous throughout`
- **Measure it** — always · `the neck extends horizontally about six metres just above the desktop`
- **No awareness** — comedy · `no awareness that his anatomy is unusual`
- **Pan along the impossible** — reveal · `start on the body at the left, one steady pan right along the neck, end close on the head at the monitor`

### A5. Material / painted / puppet characters (torn-canvas girl, paper, clay, porcelain)
- **Construction spec** — always · `a hollow figure assembled entirely from torn pieces of an oil painting: visible canvas weave, cracked paint, ragged white primer edges, lifted corners, tiny gaps; joints connected by narrow canvas strips and loose threads; open joints show the hollow interior`
- **No living tells** — always · `painted static eyes; no real skin, real hair, breathing, blinking, speech or facial animation`
- **Never a human in costume** — always · `she must never become a human wearing a costume or a human face covered with a cracked-paint filter`
- **Material motion** — walking · `stiff, uneven, puppet-like: slight jerks, delayed joint motion, loose wrists, feet placed awkwardly; panels swing as semi-rigid sheets`
- **Don't infect the world** — always · `do not apply her puppet-like movement to the camera, pedestrians, waves or birds`
- **Material reacts to the environment, subtly** — water/wind/fire · `waves wet the hem; the canvas darkens, frayed edges soften, small pigment trails spread into the water; local and subtle, no sudden disintegration`
- **Head pivots on its material** — turns · `she turns her head stiffly, slightly uneven, as though it pivots on canvas strips`

### A6. Levitation / one deliberate exception to gravity
- **Already floating at the cut** — always · `at the cut she is ALREADY levitating; both feet off the floor; the lowest shoe ~1.2 m above the rug; establish the complete floating silhouette from the very first frame`
- **Prove the air gap** — always · `a broad visible gap of empty air between her and the floor; a soft separate shadow far below with the rug pattern visible between shadow and feet; no chair, rope or support`
- **Micro-drift, not bobbing** — always · `her whole body drifts vertically by only one or two centimetres`
- **Only one exception** — always · `only she levitates; visitors stay grounded, the rug lies flat`
- **Lock the end state** — always · `the final frame still shows her floating; she never stands, lands or straightens`
- **Override the sheet pose** — when using a character sheet · `the sheet controls identity and clothing only; replace its standing pose completely with the airborne pose below`

### A7. Falling / action human (parkour, web-swing, fall through a fortress)
- **Body spec + mover type** — always · `165 cm, 47 kg, moves like a trained gymnast — core engaged, never ragdoll, never limp`
- **Pose dictionary per movement** — multi-move shots · `fall — calm, arms slightly out, clothes floating up · swing — arms forward gripping, legs trailing, core tight · wall push-off — both feet flat, knees deep, explode to full extension`
- **Asymmetric body timing** — falls, impacts · `chest, pelvis, arms and legs respond with different timing; one knee bends more than the other`
- **Reach → fail → protect** — falls · `a passing ledge triggers an instinctive reach; when she cannot catch it she turns a shoulder to protect herself`
- **No hero landing** — realistic falls · `she does not straighten into a controlled feet-first landing`
- **Impact chain** — landings · `she strikes the platform off balance; body compression, delayed fabric, a burst of dust, a short slide slowed by friction; she stays down, stunned`
- **Recovery with weight transfer** — after impact · `a trembling palm plants, a knee comes underneath, weight transfers through hand, knee and foot; she pauses partway to balance`
- **Contact at every opportunity** — parkour · `feet push off walls, body grazes fire escapes, feet skim a car roof — every contact: knees absorb, muscles engage`
- **Avoid loops** — tumbling · `no rigid spinning, no repeated identical somersaults`

### A8. Face recast / identity replacement on existing footage (video-to-video)
- **Master video pixel lock** — always · `the reference video is the master: every pixel outside the face area stays identical, 1:1, in every frame`
- **Define the target region** — always · `only her face — forehead below the hat edge, eyes, brows, nose, cheeks, lips, chin — is replaced`
- **Image = geometry only** — always · `take from the face image only facial structure; ignore its colours, grade, white balance, lighting, hair, clothing, background`
- **Lock list with colours** — always · list every garment/prop and its colour: `suit pale yellow with thin white stripes — stays pale yellow`
- **Weather and effects are footage, not noise** — rain/fog/sparks · `every rain streak, the lightning flash, splashes and mist stay exactly as in the reference; do not denoise, smooth or stabilise them`
- **Performance copied frame by frame** — always · `expressions, blinks, gaze and mouth movement are copied frame by frame onto the new face`
- **Skin takes the shot's light** — always · `the new face takes each shot's light direction, shadows and colour temperature, not the face image's light`
- **Faceless shots untouched** — always · `shots without a visible face stay completely unchanged`

## B. CAMERA
- **Name the rig** — always · `a dolly at seat height`; `operator lying on the floor`; `drone following from behind`; `fixed outside the window`
- **Camera arc** — showpieces · `static hold → trigger → violent transition → sustained travel → stillness`
- **Hold on the empty frame** — after an exit · `the camera holds on the empty window for half a beat — wind, silence — then dives after her`
- **Event → response → recovery** — every move · `a whip pan answers the head turn and settles`; `the camera flinches as the TV lands, then holds`
- **Camera mirrors emotion** — drama · `the hand tremor is nervous at the start, settles when she steps in, ends almost still`
- **Forbidden moves named** — every shot · `no zoom, no pan, no tilt`; `locked-off, no shake or reframing`
- **Measured cant** — action · `horizon cants 40–70° through the swings, levels at the peak`
- **Wide enough for body AND world** — action · `always wide enough to show her and the environment`
- **Tracking with parallax** — side tracking · `the subject stays the same place and scale in frame; near balusters pass fast, distant buildings slowly`
- **Long-lens observation** — intimacy at distance · `far across the lawn on a long lens; blurred grass foreground, characters sharp mid-ground, pond soft behind; slight long-lens shake`
- **Dolly-out reveal** — reveal the audience/context · `walks backward a few steps to reveal the bench, the dog and the branch`
- **Insert mid-action** — clarity in chaos · `close on the gloved hand, fingers open, wrist snaps — then pull straight back wide`
- **Rise to the face** — tender payoff · `holds still through the work, then one slow smooth rise up her hands and cardigan to her face as she drinks`
- **Grazing macro along a stroke** — craft details · `close to the canvas at a grazing angle, gliding with the brush; focus on the bristles and wet paint`
- **Placeholder video = camera only** — when given blocking footage · `use the video only for camera paths, framing, cuts, timing, speed and the approximate travel of her centre of mass; replace the placeholders completely`
- **Speed ramp shared by everything** — slow-motion beats · `smoothly enter brief quarter-speed slow motion, then regain speed; body, cloth, particles and architecture share the same time change`
- **Speed from near passes, scale from slow far** — vast environments · `distant structures drift slowly at different rates; speed comes from her descent and structures passing the lens`

## C. PHYSICS, CONTACT, MATERIALS
- **Contact before motion** — any grab · `fingers close around it before it moves; release only after it settles; it stays at its new position`
- **Weight you can see** — heavy objects · `a 20 kg box: tips with inertia, lands flat with a thud, bounces a few millimetres and settles, the rug compressing`
- **Real liquid** — pours/splashes · `real splash, real spinning surface, real drops; the last drop falls back into the cup`
- **Paint only where touched** — painting/drawing · `paint appears only where the bristles touch and follows them exactly; the first stroke stays as it is`
- **Paint texture** — painting · `a glossy ridge of paint rolls along the bristle edge; the stroke behind thins into dry streaky texture where the weave shows through`
- **Real mechanics of machines/instruments** — always · `tires stay grounded, pedal strokes drive the wheels, the skirt stays clear of the spokes`; `bow hair between fingerboard and bridge`
- **Assigned hands** — exchanges · `the bottle stays in her right hand; her left hand performs every exchange; the vacuum stays at her hip, unused`
- **Prop state timeline** — continuity · `headphones start on the shoulders, pulled onto the ears before the fall, stay on`
- **Wind as named movers** — exteriors · `wind moves curls, jackets, apron, petals and the spray`

## D. PERFORMANCE
- **ACTING TASK** — every key character · situation → want + why → obstacle → where the eyes go → `play the cheerful call, not the crying`
- **Job-based acting for ensembles** — group tasks · `FIRST: her job is the sugar — eyes measure the centre before she lets go. SECOND: her job is the stirring. HUMAN: her job is to hold the cup still`
- **Shared unspoken direction** — ensembles · `the morning tea is made the way it is made every morning, and nothing goes wrong`
- **Look-up for approval** — warmth · `at the end she looks up at the human's face: is it right? — answered with a small nod of thanks`
- **Already in progress** — every opening · `tears already on her cheeks`; `already delivering the end of a story`
- **Micro-arc inside 4 seconds** — close-ups · `the smile holds → melts into concentration, lips close, slow breath in → a quick happy laugh through the nose as the stroke lands`
- **Gaze lock** — always · `she never looks at the camera; her eyes stay on the canvas`
- **Off-screen action read on the face** — close-ups · `the brush stays out of view; her head turns slightly with the movement of her arm`
- **Tone guardrails** — gentle scenes · `graceful and playful, never aggressive — no stabbing, jabbing or slashing`
- **Fear that doesn't reset** — action · `fear carries across cuts without restarting; expressions change in response to events; no frozen open mouth`
- **Searching eyes before the head** — reactions · `her eyes shift toward the disturbance before her head turns`

## E. VOICE AND SOUND
- **SFX score with a silence** — showpieces · ambience → foley → line → wind → impacts → laugh → `silence at the peak`
- **Varied screams** — fear · `each scream has a different onset, intensity and ending; she inhales between bursts; her voice cracks under strain`
- **Distance-dependent voice** — wide vs close · `close: clearly audible over the wind; wide: distant, with reflections`
- **Vocal ties to the body** — always · `mouth, jaw, throat and chest move with each sound; breath effort matches every vocalisation`
- **Non-verbal emotion on the timeline** — action/comedy · `a quick surprised laugh as her feet skim the car; "WOOHOO!" echoing between buildings`
- **Small-world sound** — miniatures · `high hum of tiny wings, a clock ticking somewhere, one soft sip`
- **Space as sound** — interiors · `the reverberant hush of an empty theatre, light footsteps on old boards, a faint creak of the stage`
- **Sound changes at each cut; live music only in its own scene** — sequences

## F. LIGHT AND INTEGRATION
- **Light as a time, with a NOT** — always · `a regular Tuesday 6 pm, overcast-leaning — NOT golden hour`
- **Shadows agree** — always · `all characters cast shadows in the same direction`
- **Mixed sources on the face** — night/evening · `cool skylight on her face, warm shop-window glow on one cheek`
- **Light catches the magic, no glow** — fantasy · `the steam and the wings catch the window light; no magic glow, no sparkles`
- **Composite integration** — inserted characters · `she shares the crowd's exposure, light direction, depth of field, grain and haze; never brighter, sharper or more saturated; no spotlight, halo or pasted-on look`
- **Backlit edges** — sunset · `warm backlight catches the torn edges and loose threads`
- **Exposure event** — flashes · `exposure jumps once with the flash and settles`
- **Passing light reveals the face** — dark action · `passing light reveals her face intermittently so expressions stay readable`
- **Occlusion and reappearance** — crowds · `a close, soft-focus passer-by blocks her for a second; she reappears still on the same path`

## G. WORKFLOW MODES
- **Start-frame image-to-video** · `the video begins exactly on this frame … keep hair, outfit, set, light and colour exactly as in it`; keep 4 s clips to two beats (0–2 s, 2–4 s)
- **What we see block** — tight framings · `WHAT WE SEE: only her face and, at the left, the soft blur of the easel; the brush head never enters the frame`
- **Video-to-video recast** — see A8
- **Placeholder / blocking video** — see B "Placeholder video = camera only"
- **Reference role split** — always · one line per reference: what it controls and what it does NOT (`the person in this image is not a character reference`)
- **Multi-scene edit** — absolute cut list, one continuous take per scene, global reference numbering, GLOBAL closing block
- **Never show the sheet** — character sheets · `begin directly inside the scene; never show a reference sheet, turnaround or studio background; only one instance of her per scene`
- **Environment with no edges** — vast worlds · `structures extend above, below and beyond every frame edge; no sky, no empty horizon, no boundary`

## H. NEGATIVE BLOCK TEMPLATES (pick the lines that apply)
- **Gentle craft**: no aggression (no stabbing, jabbing, slashing); no turning to the camera; no cuts or zoom jumps; no slow motion; no paint where the brush hasn't touched; no change to hair or outfit; no text.
- **Close-up face**: no prop head in frame; no looking at camera; no posed or exaggerated faces; no smoothed or plastic skin; no change to face, freckles, makeup, earrings or hair.
- **Miniatures**: no doll or figurine look, no cartoon look, no sparkles, no magic glow, no wrong scale.
- **Material character**: no living eyes, no real skin or hair, no human in costume, no porcelain/plastic/metal joints, no glow, no mismatched sharpness, no multiple copies.
- **Recast**: no recolouring, no new grade, no new makeup, no beauty filter, no added people, do not remove rain, lightning, splashes or mist.
- **Action**: no ragdoll, no floating limbs, no repeated somersaults, no hero landing, no camera teleporting, no music.
