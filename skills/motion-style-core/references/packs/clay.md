# STYLE PACK · clay

## 1. IDENTITY
- **Name:** `clay`
- **Mood:** friendly, tactile, toy-like, calm
- **Purpose:** metaphorical explainers, onboarding, product "how it works" pieces
- **Origin:** MG-SOFT3D from tutorial 1

## 2. TYPE
| Role | Typeface | Weight | Size | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | geometric grotesque (e.g. Poppins / Jost) | Medium | 56 — **modest** | mixed |
| Deck | same | Regular | 20 | mixed |
| Label | same | Medium | 18 | mixed |
| Numeral | same | SemiBold | 40 | — |

Typography is sparse and doesn't shout: in this pack, the objects speak, not the letters.

## 3. PALETTE
| Role | Hex | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| BASE | `#FDF3E7` | seamless pastel backdrop, soft peach→cream gradient |
| INK | `#4A4038` | text, never pure black |
| DECK | `#8A7E72` | secondary text |
| STRUCTURE | — | no axes or grids, slot empty |
| ACCENT | `#F5D9C8` | the hero object, stands out through **size and position**, not saturation |
| STROKE | — | no outlines at all, slot empty |
| MARKER | `#B7D9B0` | soft green — pill labels, steps |
| MUTED | `#EDE4D3` | clay-cream, everything else |
| *extra* | `#A8C5E0` | soft blue, tertiary |

Matte, low saturation. No neon, no metallics.

## 4. SURFACE
Seamless pastel studio backdrop, soft global lighting, **no hard shadows**.
No grain. No paper texture. At most, a very soft ambient occlusion.

## 5. EDGE
Soft matte bevel: rounded edges, gentle AO, **no sharp highlights**.
Do not apply displacement filters at all.

## 6. DEPTH
True volume. A maximum of 3 main objects per frame; everything else is removed.

## 7. MOTION VOCABULARY
- **Allowed:** ENT-03 (squash-and-stretch on landing) · object falls with a bounce · two parts snap
  together · rotation on a turntable · CAM-01/03/05
- **Forbidden:** FIN-01/02/03/04/05 (all paper textures), ANN-01/03 (marker and underlines),
  FIN-06 alert wash
- **Pace:** "clay settling" — a spring with a soft overshoot and physical wobble on exit. A bounce
  **is appropriate** here, unlike in paper-vox

## 8. NEGATIVES
No photorealism. No hard highlights or shadows. No grain or print textures. No more than 3 heroes
per frame.

## 9. STAGE
Empty pastel studio sweep, softly lit, no objects.
