# STYLE PACK · blueprint

## 1. IDENTITY
- **Name:** `blueprint`
- **Mood:** precise, technical, drafting-table calm
- **Purpose:** "how it works" pieces, engineering breakdowns, assembly diagrams
- **Origin:** MG-BLUEPRINT from tutorial 1

## 2. TYPE
| Role | Typeface | Weight | Size | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | technical lettering face (e.g. Saira Condensed / Jost) | Medium | 64 | CAPS |
| Deck | same | Regular | 22 | mixed |
| Label / dimension | monospace (e.g. IBM Plex Mono) | Regular | 18 | — |
| Numeral | monospace | Regular | 28 | — |

Examples: H1 "ASSEMBLY VIEW", dimension "142mm ±0.5", stamp "REV. 03".

## 3. PALETTE
| Role | Hex | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| BASE | `#1B3A6B` | blueprint blue, the entire plate |
| INK | `#FFFFFF` | **lines and text only, never fills** |
| DECK | `#FFFFFF` @ 70% | secondary captions |
| STRUCTURE | `#FFFFFF` @ 25% | graph-paper grid |
| ACCENT | `#FFFFFF` @ 100% + heavier line weight | the hero stands out through **weight, not color** |
| STROKE | `#FFFFFF` | leader lines, dimension arrows |
| MARKER | — | the pack is strictly two-tone, slot empty |
| MUTED | `#FFFFFF` @ 40% | everything that isn't the hero |

**Key distinction:** hierarchy is built with line weight and dashing, not color.

## 4. SURFACE
Flat blueprint blue, with a faint graph-paper texture on top, opacity 8–12%.
No grain. No paper-scan texture.

## 5. EDGE
Precise ruled line: clean thin strokes 1.5–2px, no jitter, no displacement.
Construction lines use a 6/4 dash.

## 6. DEPTH
Flat, but with isometric and exploded views. Depth reads through the exploded view, not through
depth of field.

## 7. MOTION VOCABULARY
- **Allowed:** ENT-01/08 · VAL-04/06 · ANN-03/04/07 · CAM-01/03 · lines draw themselves stroke by
  stroke · exploded parts converge and assemble · the camera slowly travels along the diagram ·
  a callout circle zooms into a node
- **Forbidden:** FIN-01/02/03/04 (all paper textures), ENT-03 (spring with overshoot),
  FIN-06 alert wash
- **Pace:** "a part settling into a slot" — steady motion, a hard stop with no bounce

## 8. NEGATIVES
No color fills beyond the two tones. No photorealism. No glow. No grain.
