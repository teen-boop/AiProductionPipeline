# ARCHETYPE · torn-stack

## 1. IDENTITY
- **Name:** `torn-stack`
- **Mood:** poster, cover, graphic, minimal detail — one strong
  image instead of a page of text
- **For source:** one quote/statement/headline, when a poster-like,
  not archival, effect is needed — a thumbnail, a cover
- **Origin:** sample reference (a book cover made of torn newspaper scraps)

## 2. SURFACE
**A flat solid background in a saturated brand color** (not a table, not paper,
not a grid) — the only archetype in the genre without a photographed surface.
On it — 3–5 torn newspaper fragments of different scales and angles, loosely
overlapping each other, some running off the edge of the frame.

## 3. TYPE
- One fragment carries the **main headline**: bold condensed caps, very
  large, often cropped by the edges of its own scrap
- Another fragment — a scrap of the masthead (blackletter/serif), partially
  legible, partially torn off
- A third — a short column of text (body, serves as texture, not meaning)
- A small fragment with the date/imprint details in a corner

## 4. PALETTE
The background **is itself the accent color** (in the reference — a saturated red).
The scraps are b/w newsprint, with no color of their own. Here red is not a
mark on top but **the whole plane** — the only archetype where the accent
works not as a §6 device but as the base.

## 5. IMAGERY TREATMENT
Halftone b/w, small fragments of faces/objects inside the scraps — not the hero
of the frame, but a detail that confirms the authenticity of the clipping.

## 6. ANNOTATION DEVICE
**No hand-drawn mark.** Here the semantic accent comes from the composition
itself: the overlap, scale and rotation of the scraps. This is the only archetype
without a highlighter/wax pencil.

## 7. LAYOUT COMPLEXITY
Low in text (one short headline), but high in composition —
3–5 fragments must be arranged correctly without turning them into a mess.

## 8. TEXT FIDELITY RISK
Low for the headline (large, short — exactly what the genre demands).
Medium for the body-text fragment — the same fix: illegible texture,
a ban on lorem ipsum.

## 9. FILL-IN PROMPT TEMPLATE
```
Generate ONE image, 9:16 portrait, 2K.

SCENE: {N} torn newspaper fragments of clearly different sizes, scattered
and overlapping at different angles on a flat solid {ACCENT_COLOR}
background — no table, no paper texture beneath, no shadow gradient beyond a
small soft drop shadow under each fragment. Every fragment has ragged
hand-torn edges, never scissor-cut straight lines.

The largest fragment, roughly centered, carries one bold condensed all-caps
headline reading "{HEADLINE}", cropped by its own torn edge on at least one
side. A second, smaller fragment near the top carries a partial masthead in
blackletter or serif reading "{MASTHEAD_FRAGMENT}", torn so only part of it
is legible. A third fragment carries a short column of body text treated as
illegible grey typographic texture. A fourth small fragment in a corner
carries a short date fragment "{DATE_FRAGMENT}".

STYLE: black-and-white halftone newsprint on every fragment, visible print
grain, matte, no gloss. The background is the ONLY colour in the frame.

CRITICAL: the headline and masthead fragment must render exactly as given,
no misspellings. Body text fragment must be illegible texture only — no
lorem ipsum, no invented words, nothing resolves into real letters. No
watermark, no logos, no additional text anywhere else in the frame.
```
