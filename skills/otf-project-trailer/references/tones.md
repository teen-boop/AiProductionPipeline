# Tone presets

A tone changes pacing, cutting, typography and the music shape. It never changes the project's look: the style lock wins over
any tone. A freeform direction from the user ("like a 70s disaster film trailer", "quiet like a museum") is mapped to the
nearest preset for timing and kept word for word in the plan.

| Tone | Feels like | Pacing | Transitions | Titles | Music shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `epic` (default for drama and history) | Scale, fate, weather | Long world holds, hard acceleration in the escalation, one big peak | Hard cuts, one overexposure flash at the turn | Wide-tracked serif capitals, small | Slow build, one drop on the peak, held last chord under the title |
| `intimate` | One person, one choice | Few shots, 2-4 s holds, faces close | Hard cuts, dip-to-black breaths | Light serif, mixed case | Piano or strings, no hits |
| `mystery` | Something is wrong | Slow, withheld; the teaser shape fits | Cut to black on motion | Small grotesk, wide spacing | Drones and a pulse; silence as a weapon |
| `elegiac` | Loss, memory | Mood-piece shape, 5-10 s holds | Dip-to-black breaths, a white-out ending | Typed small title on black | A bed that never peaks |
| `documentary` | Evidence, record | Steady 1.5-2.5 s shots, fact cards on holds | Hard cuts only | Condensed sans or mono for fact cards, serif for the title | Restrained bed, a pulse under the evidence |
| `kinetic` | Momentum, action | The fastest escalation, flurry before the peak | Hard cuts, flash at the turn | Bold condensed sans, still small | Driving rhythm, beat-locked cluster |

## Pacing per tone

| Tone | Picture shots per 30 s | Shortest shot | Longest hold | Intertitles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `epic` | 15-22 | 0.4 s (peak only) | 3-4 s | 0-2 |
| `intimate` | 8-14 | 0.8 s | 4-6 s | 0-1 |
| `mystery` | 6-12 | 0.6 s | 6-9 s | 1 (the question) |
| `elegiac` | 4-8 | 2 s | 10 s | 0 |
| `documentary` | 12-18 | 0.6 s | 3 s | fact cards, up to 3 |
| `kinetic` | 20-28 | 0.3 s (flurry) | 2.5 s | 0-2 |

## Share copy voice per tone

- `epic`: one large statement and the release line.
- `intimate`: one quiet sentence about the person, never the plot.
- `mystery`: a question.
- `elegiac`: a fragment.
- `documentary`: the one verified fact, then what the film asks.
- `kinetic`: short, present tense.
