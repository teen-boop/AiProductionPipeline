# Trailer structures

Six shapes. Pick one in the plan, write why in one line, then fit the beats to the measured music (see `edit-rules.md`).
Timings are starting points: move a boundary to the nearest bar line of the track, never by more than about 10 %.
Shot counts are picture shots, not counting title and end cards.

Every structure obeys the same five laws:
1. **In motion from frame 1.** No fade-in from black, no logo, no studio card first.
2. **One promise.** The trailer sells one question or one feeling, the one the logline names. Everything else is texture.
3. **Show, then name.** The title comes after the picture has earned it, never before the first story image.
4. **No spoilers past the midpoint** of the film or season unless the user says so.
5. **The ending is a hold, a light event or a cut to black on motion** that leads into the title. Never a logo slam.

---

## 1. Full trailer (default; 30, 45 or 60 s)

| # | Beat | 30 s | 45 s | 60 s | What it does | Cutting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold open hook | 0-3 | 0-4 | 0-5 | One strong moving image, already a full composition. A detail or a gesture, not the establishing wide. | 1 shot, 1.3-3 s, no text |
| 2 | World | 3-8 | 4-12 | 5-15 | Place, period, light. Wide, then detail, then wide. | 2-4 shots of 1.5-2.5 s |
| 3 | Hero | 8-13 | 12-19 | 15-25 | The protagonist or subject. Backs, hands, silhouettes; one clear face at most. | 2-4 shots, 1.2-2 s |
| 4 | Stakes / escalation | 13-21 | 19-31 | 25-41 | The threat or the want. Shots shorten from about 1.2 s to 0.6 s; scale alternates. One intertitle allowed on a hold. | 5-10 shots |
| 5 | Rhythm break | 21-22.5 | 31-33 | 41-43 | Music drops out. Black, or one held image, plus at most one isolated sound from the world. | 0-1 shot, 0.8-2 s |
| 6 | Peak | 22.5-26 | 33-39 | 43-51 | The fastest cluster (0.4-0.6 s shots, cuts on the beat grid), then the single biggest image, held. | 4-8 shots + 1 hold |
| 7 | Title card | 26-28.5 | 39-42 | 51-55 | The title, set in code, on black or in the negative space of the last hold. | card |
| 8 | Release / end card | 28.5-30 | 42-45 | 55-60 | Date, platform or "coming soon" in the version's language. Small. | card |

Picture shots: 30 s about 15-22; 45 s about 22-30; 60 s about 28-40.

## 2. Teaser (15 s): one image, one question

The whole teaser is ONE situation. It works because it withholds.

| # | Beat | Seconds | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The image | 0-9 | One living clip (or 2-3 angles of the same instant joined by hard cuts or one dip). The camera moves 3-8 % of the frame; one layer moves inside it (smoke, water, light growing). |
| 2 | The question | 5-9 (over the image) | One short line in negative space, fades in 0.5 s, holds at least 2.5 s. A question or a fragment, never a sentence of plot. Optional. |
| 3 | Break | 9-10 | Dip to black or cut to black on motion; the music drops. |
| 4 | Title | 10-13 | Title, typed or faded on black. |
| 5 | End card | 13-15 | Date or "coming soon". |

Picture shots: 1-3. No montage. If the project has no clip that can carry 9 s, request one (`missing-shots.md`).

## 3. Mood piece (20-40 s)

No plot beats. One feeling, held. Built on "one moment, many angles" or on a rotation through 3-5 recurring places.

| # | Beat | Share | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First hold | 0-30 % | The strongest living clip, held 5-10 s. |
| 2 | Variations | 30-80 % | 2-4 more holds of 4-8 s, joined by dip-to-black breaths (one every 8-10 s) or hard cuts. Camera height or distance changes in one direction across them. |
| 3 | Fade and title | 80-100 % | Picture fades to black over about 2 s; a small title types on in the last frames of the fade and holds 3-4 s. |

Picture shots: 3-6. Music: a bed with no hits; no beat-locked cuts.

## 4. Documentary trailer (30-60 s): fact cards and archive-like frames

For non-fiction and history projects. The project's frames stand in for archive; the fact cards carry the authority.

| # | Beat | Share | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Date-and-place open | 0-10 % | An archive-like frame (the style lock's period treatment) with a small fact card: place, date. |
| 2 | Context | 10-30 % | 3-5 frames of the world. One fact card with ONE verifiable number. |
| 3 | The question | 30-45 % | What the film investigates, as a short line or a detail shot (a document, a hand, an object). |
| 4 | Evidence escalation | 45-70 % | Details and faces of the period, shortening shots, a second fact card. |
| 5 | Rhythm break | 70-75 % | Silence and black, or one object held. |
| 6 | Revelation line | 75-85 % | The strongest true sentence of the film, on a hold. |
| 7 | Title + release | 85-100 % | Title card, then end card. |

Rules:
- Every fact on a card is checked with `period-fact-check` (or the project's verified notes) and its source is written in the plan.
- At most 3 fact cards; each holds at least 1.5 s plus 0.3 s per word.
- Never invent a quote, a name or a number. Never present a generated frame as authentic archive footage: no fake archive
  stamps, reel numbers or library watermarks. If the project policy asks for it, add "reconstruction" to the end card or the
  share copy.

## 5. Series trailer (45-60 s): the season arc in three beats

| # | Act | Share | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The promise | 0-30 % | The world and the season's question. Cold open + world from episode 1. |
| 2 | The escalation | 30-75 % | A montage across episodes, at most two shots per episode, ordered by rising stakes (not necessarily by episode). Shots shorten as it goes. |
| 3 | The stakes | 75-100 % | The finale's stakes without the outcome, a rhythm break, the peak image, title, end card with the release cadence ("new episode every Friday", "10 episodes"). |

No "Part N/M" or "Episode N" labels over the picture. Episode count and cadence belong on the end card only.

---

## 6. Making-of trailer (20-45 s): the pipeline itself

For a trailer about how the project was made with AI. The escalation is the transformation, not a plot.

| # | Beat | Share | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finished image first | 0-15 % | The best moving shot of the project, in motion. |
| 2 | Rewind | 15-35 % | The same shot as reference, then as approved still, then as clip (hard cuts or one overexposure flash between stages). |
| 3 | Scale | 35-75 % | A fast cluster of other finished shots, one per scene, palette-locked. |
| 4 | Peak + title | 75-100 % | The biggest shot held, title, end card. |

For anything that must EXPLAIN steps, use `otf-presentations` instead; a trailer shows, it does not teach.
