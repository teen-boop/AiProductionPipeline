Pipeline Motion Gallery

Every move the pipeline uses, playing live. Film moves are reusable title and cut helpers for HyperFrames films; library blocks are the motion prompts from PROMPT-LIBRARY.md; board effects are BRD-01 to BRD-18 from BOARD-RECIPES.md. New: kinetic type, transitions, texture looks and AI camera moves gathered from 2026 motion trends, each with a build note or a ready video-prompt block. Pick by eye, then copy the recipe into the prompt or the composition. Motion is paused because your system asks for reduced motion; press Play on any plate.

Timings are shown at film speed. In a composition every move sits on the paused master timeline (data-start / data-duration), so the same time always gives the same frame. Graphics that follow a voice-over enter on the start of the word that names them and hold at least max(1.5 s, 0.6 s + words / 3.2). One hero effect per board; pop and rise for the rest.
